PRIVACY POLICY

Private by design.

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version

SpendFold processes your selected financial statements on your device. The current version does not send your statements, transactions, or other financial information to SpendFold or to third parties.

Information you provide

SpendFold lets you import supported bank and credit-card statement PDFs. Those documents may contain sensitive information such as your name, address, partial or complete account numbers, balances, transaction descriptions, dates, and amounts.

You may also create or edit information in the app, including transaction names and categories, recurring bills, subscriptions, loans, payoff scenarios, and savings goals.

How your information is processed

Statement extraction, categorization, calculations, and insights are performed locally on your device. SpendFold does not require a SpendFold account and does not request your online-banking username or password.

The current version of SpendFold does not contain advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not transmit your imported documents or derived financial information to the developer or other companies.

Storage and security

Imported PDFs and app-created records are stored in SpendFold's private app container and shared app-group container so the app and its import extension can work together. Imported PDFs are written using Apple's complete file-protection option.

SpendFold does not operate a cloud-sync service. Depending on your device and Apple Account settings, app data may be included in device backups managed by Apple. Those backups are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's privacy terms.

No security method can guarantee absolute protection. Keep your device passcode enabled, install operating-system updates, and delete statements you no longer want stored.

Sharing

SpendFold does not sell, rent, or share your financial information. Information leaves the app only when you initiate an action through iOS, such as importing a document, sharing content, creating a backup through an Apple service, or sending diagnostic information directly to Apple.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it through SpendFold or remove the app. Copies may remain in Apple-managed backups until those backups expire or are deleted according to your Apple settings.

Children's privacy

SpendFold is a general-audience financial utility and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SpendFold changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material updates are made.

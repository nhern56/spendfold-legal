When reporting a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the SpendFold screen involved, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not email an unredacted statement, account number, address, or other sensitive financial information.

Importing a statement

Download a supported PDF to Files, then import it from SpendFold's Statements page or share it to SpendFold from the iOS share sheet. Statement formats vary by institution, so some PDFs may require future parser support.

Managing your data

Use the Statements page to rename or delete imported statements. SpendFold stores app data locally and does not provide a remote account dashboard or cloud-sync service.

Financial information

SpendFold provides organizational tools and estimates for informational purposes. Calculations and categorization suggestions may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
